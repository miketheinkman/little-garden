Our mission

Every family deserves good tools for learning at home. We’re building Little Garden so a child can practice a tricky skill as often as they like, and a parent can see what clicked and choose what to try tomorrow.

Free for every family

Every lesson, learner profile, planning tool, and progress report on the official Little Garden app is free. A family with three children has the same access for each child as a family with one.

How lessons get better

A direction that sounds clear to its author can puzzle a child. Families can flag that moment in a lesson preview, suggest a change, or tell us which part to keep. We use those observations to revise lessons. A child who understands a skill and heads outside has had a good session here.

Supporting Little Garden

We welcome optional donations to help cover hosting and the work of creating and reviewing lessons. Every family gets the same tools whether they give or not.

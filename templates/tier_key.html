How lesson tiers and fit work

Each lesson has a difficulty tier within its skill. Tiers run from 1 to 5. Tier 1 introduces the basics. Tier 5 is the most advanced practice in this library. A child can be at different tiers in keyboard use and number sense.

On level
Within 0.4 of your child’s current estimate. For example, tier 2 is on level at an estimate of 2.25.
Slightly above or below level
Within one tier of that estimate. Choose above for a small stretch or below for familiar practice.
Above or below level
More than one tier ahead or behind.
Placement pending
Your child has yet to try a scored step in this skill.
Placement check-in
A short activity that samples several tiers to help find a starting point.

Fit updates with recent scored practice. After initial placement, the beanstalk celebrates the first time a skill estimate reaches a new whole tier.

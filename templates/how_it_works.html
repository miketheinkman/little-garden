How it works

For a first session, add a learner, preview one lesson and open their garden. Come back here when you’re ready to plan a week or look more closely at progress.

Set up your family

Request an invite. When your invitation arrives, open Parent registration and enter the code. Sign in and choose Add a learner. Use a nickname for each child. Each gets a separate garden, queue and record.

Click the learner’s name to change their lesson theme, accent, font or automatic spoken directions. The ☰ menu beside the name opens their planner, reports and device settings. Update your recovery email in Family garden. Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen if you need a reset.

Choose lessons

Open Library beside the learner. Filter by subject, adult involvement, tier or fit. Expand the tier key to interpret difficulty. Look for games and learning tools alongside phonics, art and math. Parent-required activities list the grown-up’s job and any materials.

Open a preview to try the questions and see another round before deciding. Return to the library and select Add to queue for today or Schedule for later. To establish a starting point first, use ☰ → Placement & reviews to queue a check or schedule a follow-up.

Plan a week

Open Lesson planner to manage future work. Choose the first available date and whether a lesson repeats. Open Family calendar for a view across learners. On each learner card, move a queued lesson to first place or remove it.

Use Practice focus to choose a subject or narrow practice to letters, operations or other targets. Select only those targets or mix in familiar ones. Preview and edit a proposed five-day plan before saving it to the calendar. Recurring assignments can keep their focus settings or follow future changes.

Play independently or watch together

Expand the learner card and choose Open their garden. Start or continue a queued lesson. A child can replay directions, turn answer sounds on or off, retry during practice or skip a question. With an empty queue, they can choose a suggestion or finish for the day.

For a child’s own tablet, open Learner devices, name the device and create a code. On that device, open the setup link, add it to the home screen, then open the shortcut and enter the code within 15 minutes. The paired shortcut opens that child’s garden for online lessons. You can revoke it from Learner devices.

Choose Watch live to follow the activity from another device. Switch between children or tile the views on a larger screen. Their devices try a direct connection and can use the server relay.

Read progress

In Skill details, expand a subject and skill, then select an objective to see the evidence behind it. Use Assignment history to look up a particular day or round and see answers, attempts and skips. The progress charts show how estimates change over time.

Open Weekly record & observations to browse finished lessons, add a dated note or print a week’s record. You can include your observations in the printout. Schedule a review when you want a fresh comparison.

Help shape a lesson

In a preview, flag what worked, what confused your child or what you would change. Be specific about the moment it happened. In Family garden → Lesson feedback, read replies, choose email updates, and use Try this round again to revisit a flagged question. Reopen it if it still needs work.
